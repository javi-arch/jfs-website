About

I am an architect based in Madrid with a strong interest in the intersection of design and technology. I specialize in BIM strategies and tools for the building industry. My everyday working environment is the Autodesk ecosystem: Revit for project development and Forma for managing projects in a common data environment. I have always relied on visual programming (Revit-Dynamo, Rhino-Grasshopper) and scripting (pyRevit) to automate tasks and build my own tools, but AI agents have changed the scale of what is possible: today I develop full-fledged Revit add-ins, mainly in C# and working with Claude Code. I believe digitalization and AI will radically transform the industry in the coming years, and I am committed to continuous learning so I can be part of that change.

I currently work in the BIM department of Corporación Vía Ágora (a developer, builder and manufacturer of industrialized building components), where we ensure efficiency and standardization in the development of residential projects. Alongside this, I work with Mercor, the expert platform used by the major AI companies, training and evaluating their agents.

Previously, I have been fortunate to work in very different contexts and at very different scales, from large futuristic developments in the Middle East to heritage rehabilitation projects for the public sector in Spain. In all of them, I have sought to put technology at the service of architecture, exploring data-driven sustainable design, prefabrication and digital fabrication, and the buildability of complex geometries.

In my free time, I like to apply my skills to small personal projects, spend time with my loved ones, learn about history, and unwind on the padel court.
